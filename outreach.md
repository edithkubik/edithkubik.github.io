## Outreach & Education

PEARL Lab is committed to sharing the excitement of planetary science with students and the general public. Through diverse outreach initiatives, we work to inspire curiosity about planetary processes and make science accessible to all.

### Educational Programs

**K-12 Outreach**

We partner with local schools to bring hands-on planetary science activities into classrooms. Students learn about meteorites, planetary formation, and the processes that shape our solar system.

**University Mentorship**

Our lab provides research opportunities for undergraduate and graduate students, offering mentorship in analytical techniques, data interpretation, and scientific communication.

**Public Engagement**

Regular public lectures and science café events introduce audiences to recent discoveries in planetary science and the methods we use to investigate planetary materials.

### Resources & Activities

- Sample collections available for classroom demonstrations
- Lab tours for student groups
- Mentorship opportunities in planetary science research
- Collaboration with science museums and educational institutions
