We're excited to launch the new PEARL laboratory website. This space serves as a hub for our research, publications, and outreach activities.

The Planetary Evolution & Accretion Research Laboratory is dedicated to understanding the physical and chemical processes that shape planetary systems. Our work spans from early solar system dynamics to contemporary planetary evolution.
