## Research Focus

Our laboratory examines the physical and chemical processes governing planetary evolution, with emphasis on early solar system dynamics and accretion mechanisms.

### Core Research Areas

**Planetary Accretion**

Understanding how planets grow from dust and planetesimals, including collision dynamics, differentiation, and thermal evolution.

**Isotopic Tracers**

Using stable and radiogenic isotope systems to constrain planetary formation timescales and parent body processes.

**Planetary Materials**

Investigating the composition and evolution of chondrites, achondrites, and other planetary materials through integrated analytical techniques.
