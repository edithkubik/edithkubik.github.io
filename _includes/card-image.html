{%- comment -%} Picture inside a fixed frame, used on Home (projects), News and Outreach. Optional settings, written next to `image:` in _data/projects.yml, _data/outreach.yml, or at the top of a news post: image_fit: contain (default) whole picture visible, may leave empty space cover fills the frame, crops the edges image_focus: which part to keep when cropping with `cover`: center (default), top, bottom, left, right, "top left", ... image_scale: size of the picture inside its frame, leaving a border: 1 (default) = full size, 0.8 = 80%, 0.6 = 60% (writing 80% also works) Unknown values fall back to the defaults, so a typo never breaks the page. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fit = include.fit | default: "contain" | downcase | strip -%} {%- unless fit == "cover" -%}{%- assign fit = "contain" -%}{%- endunless -%} {%- assign focus = include.focus | default: "center" | downcase | strip | escape -%} {%- assign scale = include.scale | default: 1 | times: 1.0 -%} {%- if scale > 2 -%}{%- assign scale = scale | divided_by: 100.0 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if scale <= 0 or scale > 2 -%}{%- assign scale = 1 -%}{%- endif -%}
